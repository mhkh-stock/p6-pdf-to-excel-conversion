# P6 — Package Note (Fiverr-ready)

**Gig title:** I will convert your PDF invoices and reports into clean Excel sheets

**What the client gets:** every line item from their PDFs in a
structured Excel workbook — Lines sheet, per-document totals, and a
Summary sheet whose totals are live formulas that reconcile against
the PDFs. No re-typing, no mistyped zeros.

## Tiers (Hashir's band)

| Tier | Price | Delivery | Includes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Basic | $20 | 2 days | Up to 10 PDFs, one layout, single workbook (lines + totals) |
| Standard | $65 | 3 days | Up to 50 PDFs, totals reconciled per document, Summary sheet with live formulas, validation report (what matched, what didn't) |
| Premium | $140 | 5 days | Up to 150 PDFs or mixed layouts, SQLite database of all extracted data, reusable pipeline script for the client's next batch, 1 revision round |

**What's NOT included:** scanned/image-only PDFs with no text layer
(OCR is a separate service — quoted on seeing a sample page);
documents in a language the parser hasn't been set up for (quoted
per batch).

**Delivery time note:** counts from the day the client uploads the
PDFs, not from order placement.

**Proof this service works:** this repo's sample — 5 invoices, 20
lines, PKR 184,457.60 reconciled to the cent, 24 automated checks
passing. The sample workbook is the demo the buyer sees before
ordering.
